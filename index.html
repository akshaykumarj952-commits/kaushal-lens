This panel uses national-level figures (UDISE+ / AISHE), because district-wise school-to-college transition data isn't publicly broken down the way PMKVY placement data is. Treat this as the national backdrop for the state you picked, not a state-specific number.

Commonly cited reasons students stop after school

  • Financial constraints / need to earn income
  • No college nearby / access & distance
  • Marriage or family responsibilities (disproportionately affects girls)
  • Low perceived value of a degree for the local job market
  • Lack of interest or preparedness after school

Reasons listed qualitatively per patterns reported in NSSO/PLFS-style education surveys. Exact percentage breakdowns were not available in this build — verify before quoting numbers on stage.
